The four categories of CAM software.

Every platform a board will be shown fits one of four buckets: accounting-first, portal-first, operations-first, or all-in-one. The pitch rarely says which. The pricing model always does.

Accounting-first tools came out of bookkeeping and bolt on a portal. Portal-first tools came out of resident communication and bolt on a ledger. Neither is wrong — but the bolt-on is where the demos go quiet.
